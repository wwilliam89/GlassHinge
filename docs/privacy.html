GlassHinge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026.

GlassHinge does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No data collected. GlassHinge runs entirely on your Mac. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connections of any kind.
  • No screen recording. The glass effect is drawn over your screen using standard system materials. Nothing on your screen is captured, recorded, or sent anywhere.
  • Settings stay on your device. Your style and preference choices are stored locally in macOS UserDefaults on your Mac only.
  • No third parties. GlassHinge shares no information with anyone, because it collects none.

If you contact us for support (for example by filing an issue on GitHub), any information you choose to include in that message is governed by GitHub's own privacy policy, not by GlassHinge.

Questions about this policy: github.com/wwilliam89/GlassHinge/issues

GlassHinge is an independent app and is not affiliated with Apple.
