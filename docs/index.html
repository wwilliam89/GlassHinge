GlassHinge Support

GlassHinge is a lightweight menu-bar app for Mac. Move or close your laptop lid and a liquid-glass overlay sweeps across the screen — your desktop blurs as if seen through folding glass, light catches the fold, and everything settles back to perfectly clear the moment the lid stops.

What you need

  • macOS 13 or later.
  • On laptops with a built-in lid-angle sensor (2019 16-inch MacBook Pro and many later Pros and Airs), the effect follows your hinge in real time while it moves.
  • On every other Mac, GlassHinge plays a smooth glass dissolve when your Mac wakes, plus a manual simulation mode so anyone can try the fold.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the effect only appear while the lid is moving?
That is by design. The glass is driven by hinge movement and settles fully clear about a second after the lid stops, at any angle. Your screen is never left blurry.

Does it work on a desktop Mac?
Yes. Open the app from the menu bar and use Play effect, or Settings → Preview & simulation to drag the lid-angle slider.

Does GlassHinge record my screen or collect data?
No. It draws a decorative visual effect over your own screen using standard system materials. It requests no permissions, has no accounts, no ads, and collects no data — ever.

Can I change the look?
Yes — three glass styles ship free (Ocean Blue, Clear Glass, Frost), with adjustable angle range and intensity in Settings.

Something isn't working. What should I try?
Quit and relaunch GlassHinge from the menu bar, then check Settings to see whether your Mac reports a lid sensor. If the problem continues, file an issue (below) with your Mac model and macOS version.
